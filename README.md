# Platform Deplatforming Runbook

A practical runbook for creators and small operators who depend on platforms they do not control. It covers how to prepare before anything goes wrong, how to recognize a reach collapse or suspension, what to do in the first hours, how to appeal, and how to rebuild. The throughline is simple: treat every platform as rented, and own the parts you cannot afford to lose.

This is operational guidance written from the position that losing a channel is a when, not an if. It is not legal advice. Platform policies and appeal mechanics change constantly, so the specifics here stay at the level of durable patterns rather than today's exact buttons.

## Who this is for

Anyone whose income, audience, or business runs through accounts on platforms they do not own: video channels, short-form feeds, marketplaces, ad accounts, payment processors, and the rest. If a single suspension would take out a meaningful share of your reach or revenue, this runbook is for you.

## The core principle

Reach on a platform is borrowed. The follower count, the algorithm's favor, even the account itself can be reduced or removed without warning, explanation, or recourse on a timeline you control. The only assets you truly hold are the ones you can export and move: a contact list you own, content masters you stored, and direct relationships off-platform. Everything else is exposure.

## How to use it

If nothing is wrong yet, work through `01`, `06`, and `07` now and set up the preparation. If something is wrong right now, go straight to `03`, then `04`. Read `05` once the immediate fire is out.
