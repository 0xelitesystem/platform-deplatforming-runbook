# Checklist

A one-page readiness and response checklist. Print it or keep it where you will find it under stress.

## Readiness, do these now

- [ ] I have an owned audience list with contacts I can export.
- [ ] I capture new contacts on every platform surface, continuously.
- [ ] I keep masters of all my content in storage I control.
- [ ] I have mapped my single points of failure for reach, revenue, comms, and payments.
- [ ] I have at least one established alternate presence, not started from zero.
- [ ] I own a domain and use it for my list and home base.
- [ ] I export my contacts on a schedule and keep redundant copies.
- [ ] I have tested that my backups actually restore.
- [ ] I record a baseline of normal reach so I can detect a throttle.
- [ ] I keep records of account identifiers and standing.

## Response, if it happens now

- [ ] Capture evidence: screenshots, notices, dates, before touching anything.
- [ ] Do not delete content, rage-message, or mass-create accounts.
- [ ] Message my owned list and alternate presence with a calm redirect.
- [ ] Identify exact scope: post, feature, suspension, or removal; one account or linked.
- [ ] Check linked accounts, payments, and logins for cascade.
- [ ] Find the appeal path and note the deadline.
- [ ] Submit one clear, short, factual appeal.
- [ ] Shift effort to rebuilding on owned ground while the appeal sits.
- [ ] After it settles, run a post-incident review and tighten preparation.
