# Templates

Reusable starting points. Adapt them to your situation and voice. Keep appeals short and factual, and keep audience messages calm.

## Appeal: wrongful action on content

> Account: [identifier]. I am requesting review of the action taken on [content or account] on [date].
>
> I believe this was an error. The action cited [policy, if stated]. This content does not violate that policy because [one or two specific, factual sentences].
>
> This account has been in good standing since [date], with no prior actions. I would appreciate a review and reinstatement. Thank you for your time.

## Appeal: account suspension, no clear reason

> Account: [identifier]. My account was suspended on [date] without a stated reason that I can identify.
>
> I am not aware of any policy violation. If there is a specific issue, I will correct it. I am requesting clarification and review. This account has operated in good standing since [date].
>
> Thank you for reviewing this.

## Audience message: announcing where to find you

> Quick heads up: my [platform] account is currently [restricted or unavailable]. I am working on it, but I do not want us to lose touch in the meantime.
>
> The most reliable way to stay in contact is right here on this list, and you can also find me at [alternate presence]. I will keep you posted here either way. Thanks for being here.

## Audience message: redirecting to owned channels for good

> Some housekeeping. To make sure platform changes never cut us off, I am pointing everything back to channels I control.
>
> This list is now the main line. [Site or domain] is the home base. I will keep posting on the platforms, but if you ever cannot find me there, come here. Thanks for sticking around.

## Internal note: post-incident review

> What happened: [one line]. When: [date]. Scope: [reach, revenue, comms, payments].
> What worked: [what saved you]. What did not: [the gap].
> Changes: [the specific preparation you are adding so the same event is survivable next time].
