# Before it happens

Everything that makes a deplatforming survivable has to be in place while your accounts are healthy. Once an account is restricted, the export tools and the audience relationships you did not build are no longer available to you. Preparation is the whole game.

## Own a direct line to your audience

The single most valuable asset is a way to reach your audience that does not pass through any platform's algorithm or approval. In practice this means an email list, and ideally a phone or messaging list as well, where you hold the contacts and can export them at any time. A follower on a platform is a person you can reach only if that platform allows it. A subscriber on a list you own is a person you can reach directly. Convert borrowed reach into owned reach continuously, not once.

## Capture contacts on every surface

Add a reason and a place for people to join your owned list everywhere you appear: in video descriptions, in pinned comments, in your profile, at the end of posts. The conversion rate per view is small, which is exactly why it has to run constantly. The list you build over a year of steady capture is the thing that lets you survive losing the channel that built it.

## Keep masters off the platform

Never let a platform hold the only copy of your work. Keep the original files, the high-resolution masters, the source projects, in storage you control. If a channel vanishes, you want to be able to repost the entire catalog elsewhere without reconstructing anything.

## Map your single points of failure

Write down every platform you depend on and what breaks if it disappears today: reach, revenue, customer communication, payments, fulfillment. Anywhere one account removal would take out a large share of any of those, you have a single point of failure to reduce. The map itself is uncomfortable and useful.

## Set up a second presence early

A backup channel started the day you are suspended begins at zero with no warm audience. A backup presence you have been cross-posting to for months, even lightly, is a place your audience already knows to find you. Establish the alternate before you need it.

## Document your account standing

Keep records: your account identifiers, the email tied to each account, screenshots of your standing and analytics, copies of any policy correspondence. If you ever need to appeal or prove ownership, the time to gather evidence is before access is cut, not after.
